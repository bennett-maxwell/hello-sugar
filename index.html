Hello Sugar is a beauty and wellness franchise specializing in full body waxing, sugaring, and laser hair removal, founded in 2015 and franchising since September 2021 through Hello Sugar Franchise, LLC of Mesa, Arizona. The system offers two models: Flagship salons (initial investment $266,042-$736,250) and Suite locations ($90,984-$127,914), with a $50,000 franchise fee, 6% royalty, and limited protected territories. The 2025 FDD reports 108 outlets open at the end of 2024 (87 franchised suites, 3 company suites, 6 franchised flagships, 12 company flagships), up from 75 a year earlier; trade press in 2026 reports 200+ locations open and developing after a 20-salon acquisition. The franchisor discloses no litigation or bankruptcy, offers no financing, and operates an affiliate ecosystem covering advertising, supplies, virtual reception, and franchisee coaching.

Flagship salon: 266042 to 736250 dollars. Suite: 90984 to 127914 dollars. Franchise fee: 50000 dollars (non-refundable, due at signing). Buildout for a flagship runs $150,000–$450,000; laser equipment about $60,000. No financing is offered by the franchisor — buyers arrange third-party funding.

Territory

Limited protected territory (not exclusive); minimum territory is the lesser of 60,000 people or a 2-mile radius around the approved location. Franchisor will not open another outlet in the Territory during the term if the franchisee is not in default; alternative distribution channels are reserved. (FDD Item 12, printed p51)
